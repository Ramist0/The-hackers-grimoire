# 🤝 Contributing to The Hacker's Grimoire

First off, thanks for taking the time to look at this! 🧙‍♂️

This grimoire is mostly my personal notebook, so it grows at my own pace. But I'm happy to get help making it more accurate and more useful, whether that's a typo fix or a whole new cheat sheet.

---

## 💡 Ways to contribute

- **Fix a mistake**: a typo, a broken link, a command that doesn't work, a wrong explanation.
- **Suggest a resource**: a good article, tool, lab or course that belongs in one of the sections.
- **Improve an existing note**: clearer wording, a missing step, a better example.
- **Add new content**: a cheat sheet, a write-up, a new topic. Please open an issue first so we can agree on where it fits.

---

## 🐛 Opening an issue

Issues are the easiest way to reach me. Before opening one, take a quick look at the existing ones to avoid duplicates.

A good issue says:

- **Where**: the file or section concerned.
- **What**: what's wrong or what you'd like to see.
- **Why** (if it's not obvious): a source or reference helps a lot.

---

## 🔀 Submitting a pull request

1. **Fork** the repository and create a branch with a short, descriptive name (`fix-web-security`, `add-nmap-cheatsheet`).
2. Make your changes. Keep each PR focused on **one topic**, as it makes review much easier.
3. Re-read your changes and check that links and code blocks render properly on GitHub.
4. Open the PR and explain briefly what you changed and why.

I'll review it as soon as I can. I might ask for tweaks or decline something that doesn't fit the repo, and that's never personal.

---

## 📂 Where things go

The grimoire is organised in numbered sections:

| Folder | What belongs there |
| --- | --- |
| `01-Web-Security` | Web vulnerabilities, recon, tooling (Burp Suite...) |
| `02-CTF` | CTF reports, HTB boxes and events |
| `03-Certs-cheat-sheet` | Cheat sheets and notes for certifications (eJPT...) |

If your contribution doesn't fit any of them, open an issue and we'll figure out whether a new section makes sense.

---

## ✍️ Style guide

- **Language**: English, to keep the repo consistent.
- **Format**: Markdown. Use clear headings and short paragraphs.
- **Commands and payloads**: always in code blocks, with the language specified when possible (` ```bash `).
- **Explain, don't just paste**: a command with a line on what it does and why beats a bare command.
- **File names**: lowercase with hyphens (`sql-injection.md`), no spaces.
- **Sources**: credit the original author when you borrow an idea, a table or a technique.

---

## 🚫 What I won't merge

To keep the grimoire useful and safe, please **don't** include:

- **Active or unretired challenges**: no flags, write-ups or walkthroughs for live CTFs, or for HackTheBox machines that are still active. Respect the platforms' rules and the work of their creators.
- **Exam dumps or copyrighted material**: no leaked exam questions, no copy-pasted course content or paid training material. Notes in your own words are welcome.
- **Anything targeting real systems**: no credentials, IP addresses, vulnerable real-world targets or data from systems you don't own or have explicit permission to test.
- **Ready-to-use malicious tooling**: this is a learning resource, not a weapon rack. Explaining how an attack works is great, shipping a turnkey tool for abusing real victims is not.
- **Sensitive personal information**: yours or anyone else's.

Everything in this repo is for **educational and research purposes only**, as stated in the [README](README.md). Contributions have to follow the same spirit: **learn how things break so you can defend them.** Stay ethical! 🤍

Whether you're a student or a seasoned pro, you're welcome here. Be respectful and patient, and assume good faith. Everyone started somewhere, and questions and corrections are part of how we all learn.
